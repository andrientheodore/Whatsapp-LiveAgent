Recommended implementation

From a signed link to a conversation

Proposed flow, dependent on supported contact identification and chat initialization.

  1. 01

    Open a signed link

    The link carries a signed token associated with the customer’s contact_id.

  2. 02

    Resolve the contact

    Your backend validates the token and retrieves the customer’s name, email, and phone.

  3. 03

    Initialize & open chat

    A supported widget integration identifies the contact, opens the window, and enters chat directly.

↗

The key dependency: SleekFlow contact identification

SleekFlow must support identifying an existing contact and starting its chat session. This page cannot directly fill or click inside the cross-origin iframe.

The newer AI Growth Widget documents a JavaScript API for opening and hiding the widget. That documentation does not establish support for identifying a contact or automatically initializing their chat.
